# Capture Cam

**Platform:** Android, iOS
**Type:** Mobile Application
**Cost:** Free

## Overview

Capture Cam is a camera application that registers photos and videos as traceable digital assets using blockchain technology. It captures, signs, and seals metadata in every photo or video.

## Key Features

- Blockchain-secured content credentials
- Automatic metadata preservation (date, time, location, pixels)
- Unique digital signatures for authenticity verification
- C2PA support for verifiable content credentials
- Tamper-evident provenance chain

## Use for Evidence

Capture Cam provides:
- Proof of when and where content was captured
- Cryptographic verification of original pixels
- Blockchain-anchored timestamp
- Verifiable chain of custody
