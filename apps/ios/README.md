# iOS Apps

Recommended iOS applications for evidence collection and management.

## Evidence Capture

| App | Purpose | Cost |
|-----|---------|------|
| [Capture Cam](../../android-apps/capture-cam.md) | Blockchain-verified photo/video | Free |
| Native Camera | C2PA support on newer iPhones | Free |

## Evidence Management

| App | Purpose | Cost |
|-----|---------|------|
| FolderSync (various) | Cloud sync options available | Varies |

## Notes

iOS has more limited options compared to Android for evidence-specific apps. ProofMode is Android-only. Consider using hardware with built-in [Content Authenticity](../../photo-video/content-authenticity.md) features if iOS is your primary platform.
