# ASR (Android Smart Recorder)

**Platform:** Android
**Type:** Mobile Application
**Cost:** Free (with premium options)

## Overview

Android Smart Recorder (ASR) is a voice recording application for capturing audio on Android devices.

## Key Features

- High-quality audio recording
- Background recording capability
- Multiple audio format support (MP3, WAV, OGG)
- Adjustable bitrate and sample rate
- Recording scheduling
- Cloud backup integration

## Installation

Available on [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nll.asr)

## Suggested Settings for Evidence

- **Format:** WAV (uncompressed) or high-bitrate MP3
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1 kHz or higher
- **Bitrate:** 320 kbps (for MP3)

## Considerations

- Check [legal requirements](../legal-considerations.md) regarding recording consent in your jurisdiction
- Quality depends on device microphone
- Consider dedicated hardware for extended recording sessions
