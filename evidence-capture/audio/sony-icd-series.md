# Sony ICD Series Digital Voice Recorders

**Platform:** Dedicated Hardware
**Type:** Physical Device
**Cost:** $50 - $300+ (varies by model)

## Overview

The Sony ICD series is a line of dedicated digital voice recorders known for reliability and audio quality.

## Common Models

- **Sony ICD-PX470** - 4GB internal, expandable via microSD
- **Sony ICD-PX570** - 4GB internal, USB direct connection
- **Sony ICD-UX570** - OLED display, S-microphone system
- **Sony ICD-TX660** - Ultra-compact, clip-on design

## Key Features

- Stereo recording
- Long battery life
- Timestamp embedding
- USB connectivity
- MicroSD expansion (most models)

## Advantages Over Smartphone Recording

- Dedicated purpose (no interruptions)
- Extended battery life
- Purpose-built microphones
- Discrete form factor

## File Formats

- **MP3** - Compressed, widely compatible
- **LPCM (WAV)** - Uncompressed, higher quality
