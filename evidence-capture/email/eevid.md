# eEvid

**Platform:** Web Service
**Type:** Certified Email Delivery
**Cost:** Pay-per-use

## Overview

eEvid provides certified email delivery with proof of sending, delivery, and content. It creates a verifiable record that a specific email with specific content was sent and received.

## Key Features

- Proof of delivery
- Content certification
- Timestamp verification
- Legal validity in many jurisdictions
- PDF evidence certificates

## Use Cases

- Sending formal notices
- Contract communications
- Cease and desist letters
- Evidence of notification
- Time-sensitive legal communications

## How It Works

1. Compose email through eEvid platform
2. eEvid sends the email and tracks delivery
3. Generates certificate with:
   - Timestamp of sending
   - Content hash
   - Delivery confirmation
   - Recipient information

## Considerations

- Cost per certified email
- Recipient does not need to use eEvid
- Check legal recognition in your jurisdiction
- Keep certificates with your records

## Alternatives

- **Registered mail** - Physical equivalent
- **Read receipts** - Less formal, can be ignored
- **Certified mail services** - Various providers exist
