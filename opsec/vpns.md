# VPNs for Evidence Gathering

## Overview

When conducting online investigations or gathering digital evidence, a VPN (Virtual Private Network) can protect your identity and prevent targets from detecting your research activity.

## When to Use a VPN

- Researching potentially hostile subjects who may monitor visitors
- Accessing geo-restricted content relevant to an investigation
- Preventing your IP address from appearing in target's logs
- Protecting your location when using public networks
- Separating investigation traffic from personal browsing

## VPN Selection Criteria

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| No-logs policy | Ensures your activity can't be disclosed |
| Jurisdiction | Some countries have data retention laws |
| Anonymous payment | Prevents linking account to identity |
| Audit history | Third-party verification of claims |
| Kill switch | Prevents leaks if connection drops |

## Operational Considerations

1. **Separate accounts** - Don't use personal VPN account for investigations
2. **Consistent use** - Connect before starting investigation session
3. **Kill switch** - Enable to prevent accidental exposure
4. **DNS leak protection** - Verify no DNS leaks (dnsleaktest.com)
5. **Server selection** - Choose servers appropriate for target geography

## Limitations

- VPNs don't provide anonymity against determined adversaries
- Some sites block known VPN IP ranges
- Does not protect against browser fingerprinting
- Account credentials can still identify you
- Traffic analysis can sometimes defeat VPNs

## When VPN Is Not Enough

For high-risk investigations, consider:
- **Tor Browser** - Stronger anonymity, slower speeds
- **Tails OS** - Amnesic operating system
- **Dedicated investigation device** - Separate from personal hardware
- **Public WiFi + VPN** - Additional layer of separation

## Legal Note

Using a VPN is legal in most jurisdictions, but consider:
- Some countries restrict VPN use
- VPN doesn't make illegal activity legal
- Evidence gathering must still comply with applicable laws
